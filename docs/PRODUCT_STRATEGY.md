# Product Strategy

## Vision

LocalOps Platform is a modular business operating system for micro-SMEs. It avoids building separate SaaS products from scratch by sharing one core CRM/operations platform and adapting workflows through vertical modules.

## Why one platform

Dentists, reformistas and pharmacies share many operational needs: contacts, tasks, documents, calendar, automations, integrations, dashboards and team permissions. The vertical-specific value comes from terminology, workflows, metrics and entities.

## First verticals

1. Construction/Reformas: strongest initial MVP candidate because it is self-contained and less regulated.
2. Dental: valuable for reminders, recall, no-shows and reviews, but avoid clinical records.
3. Pharma: useful as an auxiliary operations dashboard; avoid prescription workflows and sensitive patient data.

## Sales-led validation

Use demos and skeleton workflows to validate pain before overbuilding. Sales partners should sell tightly scoped outcomes, not unlimited customization.

## Recommended launch order

1. Core platform foundation
2. Construction/Reformas MVP
3. Dental MVP
4. Pharma discovery/dashboard MVP
5. Advanced automations
6. Deeper integrations
